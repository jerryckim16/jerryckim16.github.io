About

I'm a robotics engineer focused on the part of robot learning that decides whether a model works at all: the data and the evaluation.

I started on the floor at Apptronik, teleoperating humanoid robots to collect training demonstrations, where every episode was judged by yield. That taught me how much of a policy's quality is set before training even starts, and it led me to dig into the yield metric itself.

At Roboligent I now own the policy pipeline end to end, from collection through training and on-robot evaluation, and I've automated the slow hand-offs in between. My background spans applied math at UC Berkeley, instrumentation in the Republic of Korea Air Force and data annotation for deep learning.

Next, I'm working toward reinforcement learning for humanoid robot policies — and I'm documenting the path on this site.

What I do

The robot-learning loop I run day to day. Every stage feeds the next, and a weak link anywhere shows up as a policy that fails on the real robot.

01

Collect

Teleoperated demonstrations on real robots, with consistent operator procedures.

02

Curate

Filter, label and QA episodes so only usable data reaches training.

03

Offload

Datasets move to the training server automatically, driven by agents.

04

Train

Train policies on the curated datasets and track runs.

05

Evaluate

Run the policy on the robot, measure success, feed results back into collection.
